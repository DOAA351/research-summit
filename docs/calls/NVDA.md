Total revenue of $82 billion was up 85% year-over-year and 20% sequentially. Third consecutive quarter of YoY acceleration and 14th straight quarter of sequential growth. The $13.5 billion sequential revenue increase was a record. We ramped Blackwell systems across our diverse end customer base, from hyperscalers to model makers to AI cloud providers and sovereign customers. We returned a record $20 billion to shareholders while executing strategic investments both upstream (supply chain) and downstream (go-to-market ecosystem).

Spectrum-X (end-to-end Ethernet for AI) now larger than all ethernet network peers combined. InfiniBand grew >4x YoY (XDR). Data center computing revenue of $60 billion (+77% YoY); data center networking revenue of $15 billion (nearly tripled YoY).

Hyperscale = public cloud + world's largest consumer internet companies. ACIE = AI purpose-built data centers and AI factories across industries and countries. Edge Computing = devices for agentic and physical AI: PCs, gaming consoles, workstations, AI-RAN base stations, robotics, automotive. Posted a revenue breakdown on new platforms for the past nine quarters on the website.

Hyperscale revenue $38 billion (~50% of Data Center, +12% QoQ). ACIE revenue $37 billion (+31% QoQ; AI cloud revenue >3x YoY). Partner data centers exceeding 10 MW nearly doubled in a year, now surpassing 80 sites. Sovereign revenue +80% YoY. NVIDIA AI infrastructure deployed across nearly 40 countries ($50 trillion in GDP).

H100 rental price +20% YTD; A100 cloud pricing +~15%. Analysts forecast hyperscale CapEx to exceed $1 trillion in 2027; AI infrastructure spending on track to reach $3 trillion-$4 trillion annually by end of decade. OpenAI GPT-5.5 co-designed for/trained with/served on Blackwell. Microsoft Fairwater (most powerful AI DC) live ahead of schedule. AWS adding >1 million Blackwell and Rubin GPUs starting this year. Deepened Anthropic collaboration.

MLPerf: swept every benchmark; Blackwell Ultra highest throughput. GB300: 2.7x throughput increase and 60% cost-per-token reduction vs six months ago. Vera Rubin: production shipments H2, starting Q3; 7 chips across 5 racks; up to 35x higher inference throughput and up to 10x greater AI factory revenue vs Blackwell. Google A5X instances support up to 960,000 Rubin GPUs.

China: US government approved H200 licenses for China customers, but no revenue generated yet; uncertain any imports allowed. Consistent with last quarter, no China data center compute revenue in outlook.

Physical AI >$9 billion revenue over trailing 12 months. Uber partnership to power Robotaxi fleet across nearly 30 cities and four continents by 2028. Q1 total supply (inventory + purchase commitments + prepaids) = $145 billion.

Non-GAAP effective tax rate 16%. DSO 45 days (favorable collections; expect return to mid-50s in Q2). Record free cash flow $49 billion (up from $35 billion in Q4).

**Capital allocation:** Prioritize R&D and strategic investment. Increasing quarterly dividend from $0.01 to $0.20 per share [CORRECTED BY JENSEN IN Q&A to $0.25]. $80 billion share repurchase authorization (in addition to $39 billion remaining). Plan to return ~50% of free cash flow to shareholders this year.

FY2027 GAAP/non-GAAP tax rate 16-18% (down from prior 17-19% on geographic mix).
